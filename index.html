THE STORY OF KWASA DAMANSARA

From RRI Land to a New Urban Township

Kwasa Damansara is being transformed from the former Rubber Research Institute land into a major new township within the Klang Valley.

Its story combines a large master-planned setting with MRT connectivity, new neighbourhoods and a growing mix of residential, commercial and lifestyle development.

A defining feature is connectivity: Kwasa Damansara MRT serves the Kajang and Putrajaya lines, while Kwasa Sentral provides another MRT access point within the township.
