THE STORY OF KWASA DAMANSARA

From RRI Land to a New Urban Township

Kwasa Damansara is being transformed from the former Rubber Research Institute land into a major new township within the Klang Valley.

Its story combines a large master-planned setting with MRT connectivity, new neighbourhoods and a growing mix of residential, commercial and lifestyle development.

A defining feature is connectivity: Kwasa Damansara MRT serves the Kajang and Putrajaya lines, while Kwasa Sentral provides another MRT access point within the township.

TOWNSHIP INFRASTRUCTURE

Built With Water Management in Mind

Kwasa Damansara's township planning incorporates an integrated approach to stormwater management, with infrastructure intended to store, slow and manage runoff before it is discharged downstream.

Detention & RetentionWater-management areas help temporarily hold stormwater during heavier rainfall.
Drainage NetworkIntegrated drainage helps channel and manage surface runoff throughout the township.
Water CatchmentPlanned catchment areas form part of the wider stormwater-management approach.
Ecological ApproachSustainable drainage concepts form part of the township's broader water-management planning.

These measures are designed to help manage runoff and reduce flood impact. They should not be interpreted as a guarantee that flooding can never occur, particularly during exceptional weather events.
